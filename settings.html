Privacy Policy

What data PayTrack stores

Your income, expenses, installment plans, receipt images, and project settings are saved on your device first, using your device's local storage. The app works fully offline using this local copy.

If you create an account or sign in with Google, that same data is also copied to a secure cloud database (Google Firebase) under your account, so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or open it on another device. If you never create an account, none of your project data leaves your device.

When you share a project using a card number, the project data becomes accessible to anyone you give that card number to — treat it like a password.

Account information

If you register with a username, we store that username, your email or phone number (if provided), and a securely hashed version of your PIN — never the PIN itself — so we can verify it's you when you log in.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and display name, used only to create and identify your PayTrack account.

PIN, biometrics & security

Your app-lock PIN is stored only on your device to control access to the app. It is never transmitted anywhere in that form.

Fingerprint or Face ID login is handled entirely by your device's own operating system. PayTrack never receives, stores, or transmits your biometric data — your device simply tells the app "yes" or "no".

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (including encrypted transmission and access-controlled cloud storage) to protect your data, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Receipt scanning (OCR)

When you scan a receipt, the image is read directly on your device to pull out the amount, date, and bank details — it is not uploaded to any server for this purpose. The receipt image is only stored if you save it as part of an income/expense record, following the same local/cloud rules described above.

Feedback you submit

Feedback text, star ratings, and any screenshots you attach through the Feedback & Support section above are sent to us to help fix bugs and improve the app.

App permissions we request

Camera: only used when you choose to scan a receipt; not accessed in the background.

Photos / storage: used to attach a receipt image to a record and to save files you export, like Excel reports.

Fingerprint / biometric hardware: used only to unlock the app if you turn this on; we never receive the biometric data itself.

Internet & network state: used to sync your data to your account and show whether you're online, if you've created an account.

PayTrack does not access your contacts, location, microphone, call logs, or SMS.

Third parties & advertising

We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. The only third party involved is Google Firebase, which we use purely as our cloud database, authentication and crash/feedback backend — governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Firebase may store data on servers located outside your own country; Google maintains its own safeguards for this.

Data retention

Local data stays on your device until you delete it yourself (by removing a project, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app). Cloud data tied to your account is kept until you delete that data or ask us to delete your account, after which it is removed from our active systems within a reasonable time.
